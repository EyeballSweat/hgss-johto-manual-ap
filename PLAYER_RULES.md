# Player Rules - HGSS Johto Manual AP v0.1

This version uses permissions.

If the Nintendo DS game gives you an HM, badge, key item, or story access before
Archipelago sends the matching Manual item, do not use that progression yet.

## HM permissions

Do not use these overworld moves until received from Archipelago:

- Cut Permission
- Surf Permission
- Strength Permission
- Fly Permission
- Whirlpool Permission
- Waterfall Permission
- Rock Smash Permission

Rock Smash and Fly are useful in v0.1, but not required by the main logic.

## Badge permissions

Treat AP badge items as progression permissions, not necessarily the physical badge
you received in-game.

Examples:
- Do not use the Route 32 / Union Cave path until you have `Zephyr Badge`.
- Do not treat Ilex Forest / Goldenrod as logically open until you have `Hive Badge`
  and `Cut Permission`.
- Do not treat Sudowoodo / Ecruteak as open until you have `Plain Badge` and
  `SquirtBottle`.

## Story permissions

These are abstract progression permissions:

- Sprout Tower Clearance
- Slowpoke Well Clearance
- Burned Tower Clearance
- Lighthouse Clearance
- Lake of Rage Clearance
- Rocket Hideout Clearance
- Radio Tower Clearance
- RageCandyBar Clearance
- Dragons Den Clearance
- Kimono Girls Clearance
- Legendary Clearance
- Elite Four Access

They represent the right to move past a story gate, even when the DS game itself
cannot enforce that gate through Archipelago.

## Victory

Click `Defeat Lance` only after defeating Lance and entering the Hall of Fame.

## Recommended playstyle

Play honestly and avoid intentionally sequence-breaking the Manual logic. If you
accidentally do something early, either wait to click the check until it is in logic,
or mark it in your own notes and continue.
